THOUGHT BUFFER — APPLE SILICON MAC

1. Open the disk image and drag Thought Buffer into Applications.
2. Open Thought Buffer from Applications.
3. Click Connect Luna, paste your API key, and click Connect & verify.

This family build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. macOS may
ask you to approve it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Only do this for the download you received from the project owner.

Internet access is needed for GPT-5.6 Luna. Reading your saved collection and
writing drafts work locally. API usage is billed to the key's OpenAI project.

Your thoughts and connection are saved on your Mac in:
~/Library/Application Support/Thought Buffer/data/

The download includes no thoughts and no API key. To replace the key later,
open Connection and enter the new key. Replacing the app with an update keeps
your data. Do not delete the Application Support folder when updating.

Keep a backup of that data folder if you want a backup of your thoughts.
